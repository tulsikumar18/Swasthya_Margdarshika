About the Platform

Empowering Proactive Healthcare Decisions

The Swasthya Margadarshika merges machine learning classification with curated clinical datasets to bridge the gap between initial symptom recognition and timely professional medical intervention.

Trained on structured clinical patterns across 4,920 diagnostic instances, the system classifies 132 individual binary symptoms into 41 distinct medical conditions using Support Vector Machines (SVC).

System Architecture & Core Principles

High-Performance FastAPI

Migrated from legacy Flask to asynchronous FastAPI running on Uvicorn ASGI with strict Pydantic validation and automatic OpenAPI Swagger documentation.

Preserved SVC Model

The original Scikit-learn Support Vector Classifier is preserved with its exact 132-dimension feature ordering, ensuring 100% predictive integrity.

Intelligent AI Fallback

When users enter colloquial terms or unsupported symptoms, the system activates Google Gemini AI fallback to suggest clarifications without fabricating model predictions.
